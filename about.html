WHO I AM

I'm interested in how design meets technology.

My interest in frontend development comes from the opportunity to turn ideas into websites people can interact with. I enjoy working with layouts, colors, typography, responsive interfaces, and the code that brings them to life.

As I develop my skills, I work on practical projects that help me understand how websites are structured, how their components behave, and how to make the experience better across different screen sizes.

I believe becoming a good developer takes more than learning syntax. It also means solving problems, paying attention to detail, learning from mistakes, and being willing to improve.
